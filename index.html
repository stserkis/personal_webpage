About Spyros

Researcher at Technical University of Crete, working on quantum information theory and the foundations of quantum mechanics. Adjunct professor at Democritus University of Thrace, teaching quantum mechanics.

Previously held postdoctoral positions at the Australian National University, MIT, and Harvard, and worked as a quantum engineer at the quantum computing startup Photonic Inc. Received a PhD in quantum information theory from the University of Queensland.

Founder of Resort to Reason, a publishing and educational platform dedicated to exploring philosophical and scientific ideas.
